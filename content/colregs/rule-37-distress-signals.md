# Rule 37: Distress signals

Rule 37 is a single sentence. When a vessel is ==in distress and requires assistance==, she uses or exhibits the
signals described in ==Annex IV==. The full list, and the Canadian additions, are in the Annex IV lesson.

## Reading the rule closely

- **Both conditions matter.** The vessel must be in distress *and* need help. A vessel with a minor problem that she
  can handle herself is not in distress. If you need another vessel's attention but not rescue, Rule 36 covers it.
- **"Use or exhibit".** Some distress signals are sounds or radio alerts (used), others are things you show, such as
  flags, flares or smoke (exhibited).
- **The list is in Annex IV.** The rule itself contains no signals. Exam questions often ask "which rule" and "which
  annex": Rule 37, Annex IV.

## Never for anything else

Annex IV ==prohibits using any of the distress signals except to indicate distress and need of assistance==. It also
prohibits ==signals that might be confused== with them. A false or careless distress signal sends rescuers to the
wrong place and can cost a life elsewhere. Old flares are not for fireworks on a summer evening in Desolation Sound.

## A bridge-watch example

You are on watch on a 14 metre charter boat. The engine stops in a sheltered bay with good holding, the anchor is
down, and no one is hurt. You are not in distress; you call for a tow on the radio as a routine matter. Later, a
different day, the boat is taking water faster than the pumps can clear it near a lee shore. Now you are in distress
and need assistance, and Rule 37 points you to the Annex IV signals: a MAYDAY call, a DSC distress alert, flares and
so on.

## Common mistakes

- Thinking Rule 37 lists the signals. It points to Annex IV.
- Using a distress signal for a situation that only needs attention or a routine tow.
- Firing out-of-date flares for fun or for testing in public view.
- Mixing up Rule 36 (attract attention) and Rule 37 (distress).

## Check yourself

1. What two conditions trigger Rule 37?
2. Where are the distress signals listed?
3. What does Annex IV say about using distress signals for other purposes?
4. Your engine has stopped in a safe anchorage and no one is hurt. Rule 36, Rule 37, or neither?
