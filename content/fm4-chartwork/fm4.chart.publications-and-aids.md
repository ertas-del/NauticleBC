# Publications, lights, tide tables and notices

The chart is only part of the picture. Publications tell you about lights, tides, currents, warnings and changes. You must know which one to use for each job.

## Light characteristics

- ==F== fixed. ==Fl== flashing. ==LFl== long flash. ==Q== quick. ==VQ== very quick. ==Iso== isophase (light and dark equal). ==Oc== occulting (long light, short dark). ==Mo(A)== Morse.
- Colours: ==W== white, ==R== red, ==G== green, ==Y== yellow.
- ==Period== is the time for one full cycle. ==Height== is above high water. ==Range== is how far it can be seen.
- A sector light shows different colours in different directions.

Example: ==Fl(2) W 10s 12m 5M== = group of two white flashes, repeating every 10 seconds, light 12 m above high water, range 5 nautical miles.

## Sound signals

Fog signals are horns, bells, gongs and whistles at lights and buoys. They are described in the ==List of Lights, Buoys and Fog Signals==. Don't rely on hearing a signal. Sound can be hidden by wind and terrain.

## Publications

- ==List of Lights, Buoys and Fog Signals==: positions, characteristics, heights, ranges and fog signals.
- ==Canadian Tide and Current Tables==: times and heights of tides and current predictions.
- ==Sailing Directions==: descriptions of coasts, harbours, hazards and local knowledge.
- ==Radio aids==: VHF weather and navigation broadcasts, racons and AIS aids.
- ==Chart No. 1==: symbols and abbreviations.

## Notices

- ==Notices to Shipping (NOTSHIP)==: urgent warnings (a light out, a hazard). Broadcast and published.
- ==Notices to Mariners==: regular publications with ==chart corrections== and changes to publications.

To correct a chart, apply each correction carefully by hand, and write the notice number and date in the chart margin or correction log.

## Canadian buoyage

The system is ==red right returning==: red buoys on your starboard side when returning from sea. Lateral marks use even numbers for red and odd for green. Cardinal marks are black and yellow. Isolated danger marks are black with red bands. Safe water marks are red and white. Special marks are yellow.

## A BC example

A fisherman is entering Ucluelet at night. The List of Lights says the harbour light is Fl(2) W 6s. He counts two white flashes every six seconds and confirms the light before he enters. The Notices to Shipping for the area say a buoy is off station, and he keeps well clear.

## Common mistakes

- Using an out-of-date publication.
- Ignoring Notices to Shipping.
- Misreading the light period.
- Not recording chart corrections.
