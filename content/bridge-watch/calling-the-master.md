# When to call the master

**Study aid only.** The list comes from the STCW watchkeeping principles (section A-VIII/2, as reproduced by a
government source). The master's own **standing orders** and your company's rules may add more triggers. Follow them.

## What you need to know

The officer of the watch (OOW) is in charge of the bridge, but the master has overall responsibility for the ship. So
the principles list the situations in which the OOW must ==notify the master immediately==. The list has a catch-all
at the end: =="any other emergency, or if in any doubt"==. Calling the master early costs a few minutes of sleep. Calling
late can cost the ship.

## The list

| Situation | In short |
|---|---|
| Restricted visibility | ==restricted visibility is encountered or expected== |
| Traffic | ==traffic conditions or the movements of other ships== cause concern |
| Steering | ==difficulty in maintaining course== |
| Landfall and soundings | ==failure to sight land, a navigation mark or to get soundings by the expected time==; or land or a mark ==sighted unexpectedly==, or ==a change in soundings== |
| Breakdowns | ==engines, propulsion remote control, steering gear or any essential navigational equipment== fails |
| Radio | ==radio equipment malfunction== |
| Heavy weather | ==any doubt about the possibility of weather damage== |
| Hazards | ==a hazard to navigation such as ice or a derelict== |
| Anything else | ==any other emergency, or if in any doubt== |

The source list has ten items; the table joins the two landfall and soundings items into one row.

## Call, but also act

The principles add that calling the master ==does not delay==: the OOW must ==not hesitate to take immediate action for
the safety of the ship== where circumstances require. If a vessel is about to collide with you, the OOW alters course or
stops engines first, and calls the master as well, not instead.

## Where the rating fits in

You are not the person who calls the master, unless your ship's orders say so, but you can help the officer make the
call in good time:

- **Report early.** If visibility is dropping, a fog bank is ahead, a vessel is making you uneasy, or the ship is hard
  to hold on course, tell the officer now. Do not wait until it is clear. A report is cheap.
- **Report steering trouble.** If the wheel feels heavy or does not answer, the steering alarm sounds, or the ship will
  not hold the course, say so at once: that is "difficulty in maintaining course" and may be a steering failure.
- **Report what you see on the water:** ice, a log, a drifting object, a mark that should not be there.
- **If you are worried and the officer does not seem to have noticed,** say it again, clearly. If you are unsure whether
  you may call the master yourself, ask your officer or read the master's standing orders at the start of the trip.

## Worked example: which items apply?

A coastal ferry on a night watch in the Strait of Georgia. During one hour you see: (a) a fog bank on the horizon ahead; (b)
the steering gear alarm sounding briefly; (c) a tug and tow showing a steady bearing; (d) a buoy sighted that is not on
the officer's chart.

| Event | List item | Call the master? |
|---|---|---|
| Fog bank ahead | ==Restricted visibility expected== | Yes |
| Steering alarm | ==Breakdown or difficulty with steering== | Yes |
| Tug and tow steady bearing | ==Traffic causing concern== | Yes |
| Unexpected buoy | ==Mark sighted unexpectedly== | Yes |

Four items in one hour is a good reason for the master to be on the bridge. And even a single item would be enough.

## Exam traps

- **Restricted visibility "expected"**: it does not need to have arrived.
- **Doubt alone is enough**: you do not need proof of danger.
- Land or marks **unexpectedly sighted** and land or marks **not sighted by the expected time** are **both** on the list.
- **Radio malfunction** is a separate item from navigational equipment.
- Calling the master **does not replace** immediate action for the safety of the ship.
- A hazard such as ice or a derelict is on the list even if it is not close yet.
