# Oral: distress signals and the International Code of Signals

The oral exam asks you to recognise and explain signals. Say your answer in a short, complete sentence, then give the reason or what you would do.

## Distress signals (collision regulations)

Name them clearly: ==Mayday== by radio, ==SOS==, ==red flares and parachute rockets==, ==orange smoke==, ==flames== on the vessel, ==raising and lowering outstretched arms==, a ==square flag with a ball==, ==continuous fog signal==, a ==gun== fired at intervals, the code signal ==NC==, ==EPIRB==, and a ==dye marker==. Say they are used ==only in grave and imminent danger==, and that misuse is prohibited.

## International Code of Signals: single letters

| Flag | Meaning |
|---|---|
| ==A== | I have a diver down. Keep well clear at slow speed. |
| ==B== | I am taking in, discharging or carrying dangerous goods. |
| ==D== | Keep clear of me. I am manoeuvring with difficulty. |
| ==E== | I am altering my course to starboard. |
| ==I== | I am altering my course to port. |
| ==L== | You should stop your vessel instantly. |
| ==M== | My vessel is stopped and making no way. |
| ==O== | Man overboard. |
| ==S== | My engines are going astern. |
| ==U== | You are running into danger. |
| ==V== | I require assistance. |
| ==W== | I require medical assistance. |
| ==Y== | I am dragging my anchor. |

## Meanings for fishing vessels

Near fishing grounds some letters have special meanings:

- ==G==: I am hauling nets.
- ==Z==: I am shooting nets.
- ==P== (Blue Peter): my nets have come fast upon an obstruction.
- ==T==: keep clear of me, I am engaged in pair trawling.

## Radio distress procedure

Switch to ==VHF channel 16== (or use ==DSC==), say ==Mayday== three times, ==this is== and the vessel name three times, then give the position, nature of distress, assistance required and number of persons on board. Say what flares and EPIRB you have ready.

## Model answers

**Examiner: What signals show a vessel is in distress?** "Radio Mayday, SOS, red flares and parachute rockets, orange smoke, flames, outstretched arms raised and lowered, a square flag with a ball, and an EPIRB. They can be used only when a vessel or person is in grave and imminent danger."

**Examiner: What does flag Oscar mean?** "Man overboard."

**Examiner: What does flag Papa mean at sea on a fishing vessel?** "My nets have come fast upon an obstruction."

## Tips

Keep answers short. If you are not sure, say what you do know and how you would find out. Never guess a signal's meaning for a safety-critical situation.

## A BC example

Fishing off Haida Gwaii, a skipper hears an examiner ask about flag Zulu. He answers: "Near fishing grounds it means I am shooting nets." He adds that its normal meaning is I require a tug.

## Common mistakes

- Giving one signal and stopping.
- Mixing up the diver flag with a man-overboard flag.
- Not knowing the fishing meanings.
- Forgetting to say distress signals are only for grave danger.

## Check yourself

1. Name five distress signals.
2. What do flags G, Z and P mean to a fishing vessel?
3. What does flag A mean?
