# Oral: pollution prevention and response

The oral covers pollution as a master's responsibility. Give a practical answer.

## Fuelling precautions

Moor safely and ==stop engines and flames==. Place ==drip trays== or absorbents under connections. Know the ==tank capacity== and fill ==slowly==. ==Stop before the tank is full==. Keep a ==spill kit== ready. ==Wipe spills== and dispose of rags properly. Record the fuel taken.

## Preventing pollution

- ==Oil==: don't discharge oily bilge water. Keep the bilge clean. Fix leaks. Dispose of waste oil ashore.
- ==Garbage==: ==never throw plastic== overboard. Keep garbage and dispose of it ashore.
- ==Other pollutants==: paint, chemicals, fishing gear lost or abandoned, and sewage in sensitive areas.

## Duty to report

==Report any pollution incident immediately== to the Coast Guard through MCTS on VHF 16 or by phone. Give position, type of pollutant, amount, and what you are doing. Keep records.

## Action on board

Stop the source, contain the spill with absorbents, protect drains, call for help, and follow the vessel's emergency plan. Don't use dispersants unless told to.

## Pollution found at sea

If you see a slick or debris, note the ==position, time, size and type== and report it to the Coast Guard. Don't try to disperse it without direction.

## Model answers

**Examiner: What precautions do you take when fuelling?** "I stop the engine and any flames, put drip trays and absorbents in place, fill slowly, stop before the tank is full, wipe spills and keep a spill kit ready."

**Examiner: You see an oil slick at sea. What do you do?** "I note the position, time and extent, avoid driving through it, and report it to the Coast Guard right away."

## A BC example

Fuelling at Port Hardy, a drip falls on the dock. The skipper wipes it up at once and logs the fuel taken. Later he sees a rainbow sheen off the point and reports it to Prince Rupert MCTS.

## Common mistakes

- Overfilling at the dock.
- Pumping oily bilge overboard.
- Using detergent on a spill.
- Reporting late.

## Check yourself

1. What are the fuelling precautions?
2. Who do you report pollution to?
3. What do you do if you find a slick at sea?
