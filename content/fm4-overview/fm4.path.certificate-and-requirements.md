# The Fishing Master, Fourth Class certificate and how to get it

The Fishing Master, Fourth Class certificate is the first master's certificate for fishing vessels. This course covers the four exams. Everything here is written in original words from the syllabus in Transport Canada's TP 2293 and the Marine Personnel Regulations. Always confirm the current requirements with Transport Canada.

## What the certificate allows

The holder may act as ==master== on a fishing vessel of ==not more than 100 gross tonnage== on a ==near coastal== or ==sheltered waters== voyage. The holder may also act as ==officer in charge of the watch== on a fishing vessel of any tonnage on those voyages.

## The requirements (Marine Personnel Regulations, section 142)

- ==Sea service==: at least ==12 months== performing deck duties on one or more fishing vessels ==6 m or more== in length. Up to 6 months may come from an approved training program, and up to half the service may be on normal trading vessels of 6 m or more.
- ==Certificates== to show the examiner: ==MED with respect to basic safety==, ==ROC-MC== (the commercial marine radio certificate), ==SEN Limited==, and ==marine advanced first aid==.
- ==Exams==: Chartwork and Pilotage level 1, Navigation Safety level 1, Ship Construction and Stability level 1, then an oral exam on general seamanship after meeting the other requirements.

A seafarer's medical may also be needed. Training providers describe a fatigue-management course before MED training. Check the current rules with Transport Canada.

## The four exams

| Exam | What it is | Course here |
|---|---|---|
| ==C/P 1== Chartwork and Pilotage | practical chartwork and multiple choice, unlimited time | chart lessons |
| ==NS 1== Navigation Safety | multiple choice, 1.5 hours | collision regulations and watchkeeping |
| ==SCS 1== Ship Construction and Stability | multiple choice and calculations from a stability booklet, 3 hours, open book | stability and construction lessons |
| ==FM 4-O== Oral on General Seamanship | unlimited time, scenario questions | oral lessons |

The pass marks are not stated in TP 2293. The practice exams in this app use assumed settings and say so.

## How to use this course

1. Work through the chart, collision, stability and construction lessons and the practice questions.
2. Use the oral lessons to practise out loud. Examiners ask "what would you do if..." questions.
3. Take the practice exams for each subject near the end.
4. Use a Transport Canada approved course for the formal training. This app does not replace it.

## A BC example

A deckhand on a salmon troller out of Prince Rupert has 14 months of deck service on 12 m fishing boats. She holds MED basic safety, ROC-MC, SEN Limited and marine advanced first aid. She now works through the course, takes the four exams, and applies for the certificate through Transport Canada.

## Common mistakes

- Assuming the certificate lets you run any size of fishing vessel. The limit is 100 GT on near coastal and sheltered waters.
- Counting sea service on boats under 6 m.
- Forgetting that the oral exam comes after the other requirements.

## Check yourself

1. What is the gross tonnage limit for a master with this certificate?
2. How much sea service is required?
3. Name the four exams.
